# Provenance Policy

Every populated atlas record must be traceable to its source. The minimum
provenance pointers are:

```text
paper → figure → table → supplementary material → page
```

The software reports missing pointers and does not fill them from citation
metadata. DOI and PMID are identifiers, not substitutes for figure/table/page
traceability.

No numbers are crawled, extracted, synthesized, or inferred by this repository.
Curators remain responsible for source fidelity and unit compatibility.
