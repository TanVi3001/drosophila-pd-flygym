# Literature-driven Calibration Research Campaign

## Purpose

This document describes the planned research workflow for converting
curator-reviewed literature observations into transparent computational
calibration targets. It does not add a runtime workflow, download papers, or
populate scientific data.

## End-to-end workflow

```text
Paper registry
      |
      v
Human literature curation
      |
      v
Phenotype Atlas
      |
      v
Computational signature
      |
      v
Calibration targets
      |
      v
Calibration
      |
      v
Holdout validation
      |
      v
Publication package
```

### 1. Paper registry

The campaign begins with curator-supplied records in
[`paper_registry.csv`](../research/campaign/paper_registry.csv). The registry
is an inventory, not a citation generator. DOI, PMID, title, assay, reviewer,
status, and priority must be entered from verified sources.

### 2. Human curation

The [Literature Assistant](literature_assistant.md) may parse explicit local
files into candidate records. It does not crawl, download, infer phenotype, or
write the atlas directly. Each candidate remains pending until a human
reviewer approves, rejects, or edits it.

### 3. Phenotype Atlas

Only approved candidates with complete provenance may be exported to the
existing [Digital Phenotype Atlas](digital_phenotype_atlas.md). Atlas records
retain paper, figure, table, supplement, and page references.

### 4. Computational signature

Approved summaries can be represented as a
[computational disease signature](disease_signature.md). Missing metrics remain
`unavailable`; they are not filled with zeros or inferred from unrelated
fields.

### 5. Calibration targets

Curators record compatible metric, value, unit, source, assay, uncertainty, and
status in [`calibration_targets.csv`](../research/campaign/calibration_targets.csv).
The [calibration framework](calibration_framework.md) evaluates declared
targets only. It does not establish a biological mechanism.

### 6. Calibration and validation

The [validation plan](../research/campaign/validation_plan.md) separates
calibration data from holdout data, records missing targets, and requires
reproducible parameters and seeds. The [signature matching](signature_matching.md)
and [distance framework](signature_distance.md) define comparison semantics.

### 7. Publication

The [publication plan](publication_plan.md), [figure plan](figure_plan.md), and
[table plan](table_plan.md) describe intended outputs. They are planning
documents only; no figure, table, result, or conclusion is generated by this
campaign template.

## Governance

The research lead must approve the inclusion criteria, target definitions,
holdout split, statistical protocol, and publication claims before real values
are added. The repository must preserve both approved and rejected curation
decisions and must report unavailable data explicitly.

## Scientific boundary

This campaign evaluates computational concordance between declared literature
signatures and simulation signatures. It does not demonstrate a molecular
mechanism, replace biological experiments, or support medical interpretation.
