# Literature Curation Protocol

1. Record the source identity and citation before entering any value.
2. Record species, strain/genotype, gene/mutation, expression system, sex, age,
   temperature, assay, arena, lighting, and camera settings when reported.
3. Enter values with their original units and preserve sample size and
   uncertainty.
4. Link every phenotype to paper, figure, table, supplement, and page through
   the explicit `provenance` object.
5. Mark manual review explicitly. Do not infer missing information.
6. Validate the database before using it for calibration.

The atlas stores reported observations. It does not decide whether two assays
are biologically equivalent or whether a simulation metric is compatible with
a paper metric; that review belongs to the research protocol.
